REFINEMENT 1 — CELESTIAL GLYPHS IN THE SKY BAND
The sky band is currently just a gradient. Add the actual brand mark, matching the in-app style:
- Daytime: a soft sun — cream-gold radial halo (~35-50% opacity bloom), brighter cream core ~10-12px, positioned on the right side of the sky band
- Nighttime: a crescent — cream-coloured disc with a darker disc offset to create the crescent shape, set inside a soft cream halo, same placement as the sun
- Dynamic Island Compact: replace the generic gold dot in the leading pill with a tiny version of the same glyph (sun for day, crescent for night) at ~14-16px

This is the most important fix. The brand mark IS the light, and right now it's missing from the surface users glance at most.

REFINEMENT 2 — SKY SATURATES WITH URGENCY
Currently Normal / T-10 / T-30 share the same sky. They should deepen as the prayer approaches:
- Normal: calm sky for the time of day
- T-10: sky begins warming — daytime gets an amber tint at the horizon, night gets a hint of pre-dawn pink
- T-1: sky saturates further toward gold/sunset
- T-30s: full golden-hour saturation, sun glyph appears larger and lower in the band
- T-0: the most beautiful sky of the cycle (full sunset for day prayers, full dawn for Fajr)

Urgency is signalled by the sky deepening, NOT by red borders or alarm chrome. Keep the subtle gold border glow on T-1 and T-30 only — pull it off the other states where it currently appears.

REFINEMENT 3 — T-0 IS A DIFFERENT MODE
Currently T-0 looks like a normal countdown card with the words swapped. Restructure as a quiet, ceremonial state:
- Remove the "TO [PRAYER]" label
- Remove the large countdown numerals
- Remove the "AT [TIME]" stamp
- Centre the layout vertically and horizontally
- Italic Fraunces "Time for [Prayer]" at ~28px
- Arabic "حان وقت [الصلاة]" stacked below at ~22px in soft gold
- Optional small badge above with a glowing gold dot: "● NOW · TIME TO PRAY"
- More breathing room than the other states — let the moment land

This is the only state where the design fully releases tension. The restraint is the point.
